Spotting real trees vs crops and weeds. One photo cannot tell a green wheat field from an orchard; a year of photos can, because crops turn brown after harvest and trees stay green. We trained a model on a year of photos and tested it against the simple rules.

What factories and farmers say is checked against a source they do not control: Climate TRACE, the air satellite, the greenness satellite and the European land-cover map.
