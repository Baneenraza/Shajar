Is the smarter tree check worth it?

The simplest way to spot trees is one satellite photo and one rule: greenness above a line means trees. But a wheat field in March is just as green as an orchard. Trees stay green all year, while crops turn green and then brown after harvest. So we trained a model that looks at a whole year of photos, and tested it against the simple rules on points across lower Sindh.

Where the numbers come from

  • Factory pollution: Climate TRACE, 2025 estimates, with Climate TRACE's own confidence rating.
  • Satellite readings: Sentinel-5P (air) and Sentinel-2 (greenness) from the European Copernicus programme, read through Google Earth Engine on 11 November 2026.
  • Tree uptake: IPCC 2019 default for tree plantations in dry parts of Asia, about 6 tonnes of carbon dioxide per acre per year.
  • Farmers, plots, contracts and prices are made up for this demo. The satellite readings at those plot locations are real.
